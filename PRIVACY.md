# Inkpad privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Inkpad is a note-taking extension for Google Chrome. This policy explains what it does with data.

**Inkpad does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.** It has no servers, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising. It makes no network requests.

## What stays on your device

- **Your notes** are saved as files in a folder you choose on your own computer, or, if you haven't chosen a folder, in Chrome's local extension storage on your device.
- **Settings** (theme, font, pinned notes) and a temporary recovery copy of the note you're editing are kept in Chrome's local extension storage.
- **Web clips:** when you use "Clip selection", "Save link" or "Save this page" from the right-click menu, the selected text, page title and address are written into a note in your folder. This only happens when you choose that menu item.
- **Current page link:** when you press the link button or Alt+L, Inkpad reads the title and address of the tab you're on to insert a link into your note.

None of this leaves your device through Inkpad. Removing the extension deletes its local storage; files in your notes folder remain yours and are not touched.
