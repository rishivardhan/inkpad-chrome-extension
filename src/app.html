      Notes that live on your disk

      Pick a folder and Inkpad saves every note there as a plain Markdown file. Close Chrome, open the folder in VS Code or your file manager, and your notes are right where you left them.

      Without a folder, notes stay in Chrome’s extension storage. You can move them into a folder anytime.
